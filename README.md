## Project Overview
 

Name: Lesego's (LSG) Supermarket (local supermarket). Lesego's (LSG) Supermarket is an independent, family-run supermarket founded in 2013 serving a growing suburban community with fresh produce, groceries, and household essentials at competitive prices.  

 

Mission: To provide affordable, quality groceries with friendly, convenient service. Vision: To become the community's trusted first choice for everyday shopping. 

 

Target audience: Local households, working professionals, and elderly residents within a 10km radius who value convenience, freshness, and value for money.

## Website Goals and Objectives
 . Increase brand visibility and attract new customers online. 

 . Enable customers to view weekly specials, promotions, and store locations easily. 

 . Provide a simple enquiry channel for feedback. 

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): monthly website visits, enquiry form submissions, and time spent on the promotions page. 

## Proposed Website Features and Functionality 
Homepage: hero banner, weekly specials highlight, and quick navigation.  

About Us: store history, mission, and team introduction.  

Products/Services: categorized grocery departments and offerings.  

Enquiry page: form for feedback, supplier, or partnership enquiries.  

Contact page: store locations, maps, hours, and contact form.
