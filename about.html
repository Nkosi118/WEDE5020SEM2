From a single family-run store to two busy branches - here's our story.

Our History

Started by one family, grown by a whole community.

LSG Supermarket opened its first branch in Pretoria in 2015 as a small, family-run neighbourhood grocer. What began as a single store with a handful of staff grew steadily through word of mouth and reliable, friendly service.

Steady growth and a loyal customer base led to the opening of a second branch in 2021. Today, LSG Supermarket stocks groceries, fresh produce, household goods and a small in-store bakery across both locations.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, quality groceries with friendly, convenient service every day of the week.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and convenient neighbourhood supermarket in the greater Pretoria area.

Who We Serve

Local households, and the people who shop for them.

We serve local households and families in Gauteng doing their regular weekly grocery shopping, as well as busy professionals who value convenience and good value for money. Whatever your week looks like, we aim to make the shop quick, affordable and pleasant.
